About MERKUZ At MERKUZ, we believe that true healing begins when you finally feel at home. For many in our community, the path to wellness is often obstructed by a "Period of Rejection"—a time when clinical care feels disconnected from cultural reality. We are here to change that. By seamlessly blending clinical expertise with the profound richness of African cultural heritage, we transform isolation into a powerful sense of connection. Our approach ensures that no one has to leave their identity at the door to receive world-class care. We aren't just treating symptoms; we are fostering a homecoming where your heritage is the heartbeat of your healing. At MERKUZ, you are never an outsider in your own recovery—you are exactly where you belong.

Merkuz Holistic Mental Health and Wellness Center was founded through the lived experience of our founder, Menbere Abere, who spent over a decade as a caregiver for loved ones living with mental health conditions. Her personal journey ignited a deep commitment to support others — especially those from underrepresented communities — who are navigating similar challenges.
MERKUZ is an Amharic word which means a walking stick. From many years of experience supporting family members who were struggling with mental illness , she wittnesed that being a support like MERKUZ in their journey from acceptance to recoery was the main component.

A 2022 study by the American Psychological Association highlighted that children and families who have experienced migration-related trauma often encounter significant obstacles in accessing mental health support. Merkuz was founded to help bridge that gap.

Located in the Greater Bay Area, Merkuz was created to serve individuals and families of the African Diaspora communities. Many of these families face unique barriers to mental health care due to cultural stigma, language differences, and a general distrust of health systems.

We offer culturally sensitive, language-specific services that recognize the central role of family and community in healing. Our work addresses issues related to mental health, substance use, and emotional well-being — with a particular focus on helping immigrant communities adjust to life in the U.S. We understand the emotional and psychological challenges that come with resettlement and aim to provide the support and tools needed not just to survive, but to thrive.

04

Telehealth

Secure & Confidential Online Therapy from Comfort of Your Home

At Merkuz Mental Health and Wellness Center, we believe that taking care of your mind should be accessible, convenient, and free of barriers. Our telehealth services allow you to connect with licensed therapists, psychiatrists, and wellness counselors online through a secure video platform. Whether you are dealing with anxiety, depression, managing life transitions, or looking to improve your overall emotional well-being, our team is here to walk alongside you on your journey to healing.

Mental Health Services Available via Telehealth

  • Individual Therapy & Counseling: One-on-one virtual sessions to address personal challenges, trauma, anxiety, and depression in a safe environment.
  • Psychiatric Consultations: Secure online evaluations, medication management, and ongoing treatment planning with professional specialists.
  • Holistic Wellness Coaching: Virtual sessions focused on mindfulness, stress management, and building healthy lifestyle habits to balance mind and body.
  • Group Therapy & Support Circles: Safe, moderated online spaces to connect with others sharing similar life experiences and healing goals.

How it Works

  1. Book an Appointment: Click our online scheduler below to select a therapist and find a time that fits your weekly schedule.
  2. Receive Your Secure Link: Check your email or text messages for a private, encrypted access link to our virtual waiting room.
  3. Attend Your Session: Click the link using any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera to start your secure face-to-face consultation.

Your Privacy is Our Absolute Priority

We understand that discussing mental health requires absolute trust. All Merkuz virtual care sessions are conducted over an encrypted, HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform. Your data, identity, and conversations remain completely confidential and are never recorded.

Adults , Family and Children Behavioural Health Services

Comprehensive Behavioral Health Support for All Stages of Life

At Merkuz Mental Health and Wellness Center, we understand that behavioral health challenges affect individuals differently depending on their life stage and family dynamics. Our dedicated team provides compassionate, evidence-based behavioral health services tailored to meet the unique needs of adults, children, adolescents, and families. We are committed to fostering resilience, improving communication, and helping your entire family thrive.

Our Specialized Behavioral Health Programs

  • Adult Behavioral Health: Individualized care focusing on stress management, trauma recovery, mood regulation, and navigating complex life transitions.
  • Child & Adolescent Services: Age-appropriate therapy and behavioral interventions to help children and teens cope with academic stress, social anxiety, behavioral challenges, and emotional development.
  • Family Counseling & Dynamics: Guided sessions designed to strengthen family bonds, resolve conflicts, improve communication, and support parents with effective guidance strategies.
  • Crisis Prevention & Coping Skills: Proactive tools and personalized strategies to help individuals and families manage acute stressors and build long-term emotional stability.

Our Collaborative Approach to Care

  1. Holistic Assessment: We take the time to understand the unique emotional, social, and behavioral environment surrounding you or your child.
  2. Tailored Treatment Plans: Our specialists design a collaborative care plan that respects your family's values, goals, and cultural background.
  3. Integrated Support: We work together with parents, caregivers, and individuals to ensure consistency, growth, and lasting healing outside of session hours.

A Safe Space for Your Family to Grow and Heal

Seeking behavioral health support is a powerful step toward a healthier, more balanced life. Whether you are looking for personal guidance, worried about your child's developmental milestones, or hoping to restore harmony to your household, Merkuz provides a safe, judgment-free space to guide you forward.
